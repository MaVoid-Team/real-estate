# MaVoid Real Estate

A luxury listing site where you can browse MaVoid properties and walk through a unit in 360° before you get in touch.

MaVoid Real Estate is for people shopping for a high-end home who want to feel the space, not just skim photos — city residences, oceanfront villas, and mountain lofts included.

- Browse past projects, from downtown condos to beach villas and mountain retreats
- Step inside a premier unit with an on-page 360° preview you can look around
- Open the full tour to move between rooms — kitchen, living room, bedroom, terrace
- Zoom, rotate, and pick a view, then send a message when a listing feels right

## Try it

Live site: [https://real-estate-pearl-theta-71.vercel.app](https://real-estate-pearl-theta-71.vercel.app)

## How it works

The homepage introduces MaVoid, shows a handful of recent properties, then drops you into a panoramic preview. From there you can launch a full-screen gallery to switch rooms and look around. When you are ready to talk, the contact form is on the same page.
